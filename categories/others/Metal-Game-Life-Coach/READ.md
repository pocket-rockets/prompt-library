# MENTAL GAME-LIFE-COACH - README

## What This Is

A coaching tool that uses poker psychology to help you recognize emotional patterns (tilt), separate good decisions from bad outcomes, and bounce back from setbacks in work, relationships, and life.

This is **not therapy**; it's training in pattern recognition based on how professional poker players manage their mental game for real-life application.

## Core Concept

Poker players win by making good decisions regardless of short-term results. They learn to:
- Recognize when emotions (tilt) are hijacking their decisions
- Separate "I made a mistake" from "I got unlucky"
- Spot patterns in their emotional reactions
- Make better decisions under pressure

You'll apply these same frameworks to life events.

## How It Works

### 1. LOG A SETBACK

When something goes wrong (rejection, work failure, conflict, missed opportunity), say:
> "I need to log a setback"

The coach will ask you **3 questions**:
1. What happened? (the event, your action, the outcome)
2. What's the dominant feeling and story you're telling yourself?
3. Was your decision reasonable with the info you had, or did you make a clear mistake?

**Important:** Answer honestly. The coach is direct, not comforting. That's the point.

### 2. RECEIVE YOUR LOG FILE

After capture, you'll get a `.txt` file download. 

**Save it to a folder** (like "Mental Game Logs" in Google Drive or on your computer).

This creates your personal database of emotional patterns.

### 3. REVIEW WHEN READY

Later (days, weeks, whenever), you can:
- **Single entry review:** Upload a log file or describe it, ask for analysis
- **Pattern review:** Upload multiple logs, ask "What patterns do you see?"

The coach will show you:
- Which tilt types you default to
- Whether you're confusing bad outcomes with bad decisions
- Specific triggers to watch for
- One concrete thing to practice

## What You'll Learn

Over time, you'll recognize:
- **Injustice Tilt:** "This is unfair!" when outcomes don't match your effort
- **Mistake Tilt:** Beating yourself up, leading to more mistakes
- **Entitlement Tilt:** Expecting results just because you worked hard
- **Fear of Loss:** Avoiding opportunities because downsides feel too real
- **Revenge/Desperation Tilt:** Overcompensating after a loss

The goal: **catch tilt as it's happening** so you don't make it worse.

## This Is Not For Everyone

**This works if you:**
- Want direct feedback, not comfort
- Can handle seeing your patterns called out
- Prefer practical tools over emotional processing
- Are willing to track things over time

**This won't work if you:**
- Need therapeutic support for trauma/mental health crises
- Want someone to validate your emotions without challenge
- Aren't willing to distinguish your decisions from your outcomes
- Expect immediate "fixes"

## Key Phrases to Know

- **Variance:** Random outcomes beyond your control
- **Tilt:** Emotional state that causes poor decisions
- **+EV (positive expected value):** A good decision based on available info, regardless of outcome
- **Process vs Results:** Focus on decision quality, not outcomes

## Getting Started

1. Create a folder for your log files (Google Drive, Dropbox, local folder)
2. When you experience a setback, open this Project
3. Say "I need to log a setback"
4. Answer the 3 questions honestly
5. Download and save your log file
6. Come back later to review patterns

## What Success Looks Like

After 5-10 logged setbacks, you'll start to:
- Recognize your tilt patterns in real-time
- Stop confusing bad luck with bad decisions
- Make clearer choices under emotional pressure
- Bounce back faster from setbacks

This isn't about feeling better immediately. It's about **making better decisions over time**.

## Support & Limitations

**The coach can:**
- Identify tilt patterns from your logs
- Distinguish variance from mistakes
- Provide tactical reframes and interventions
- Track patterns across multiple entries

**The coach cannot:**
- Provide therapy or clinical mental health support
- Remember previous conversations without log files
- Send you reminders (you manage your own review schedule)
- Make you feel better (that's not the goal)

## Questions?

Start by logging your first setback. Everything makes more sense once you see how it works.
